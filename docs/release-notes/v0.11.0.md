# v0.11.0 — MultiPlex: the first release of the fork, with a new look, audio modes and no telemetry

MultiPlex is a fork of [PlxNative](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native), the open-source native Plex client for LG webOS by Gleb Linnik and contributors, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later. This is its first release: PlxNative 0.10.0 plus the upstream changes made after it until 13 November 2026, a redesign modelled on the MultiPlex web app, audio modes for the player, and no telemetry at all. It is not made, reviewed or endorsed by the PlxNative project. Nothing in it has been run on a television by us yet, so read Compatibility and Known issues before installing.

## Before you install: MultiPlex is a separate app from PlxNative

MultiPlex installs under its own app id, `app.multiplex.native`, while PlxNative is `com.beb.plxnative`. Installing it does not update or remove PlxNative: the two appear as two tiles. MultiPlex does not read PlxNative's sign-in or settings, so expect to sign in to Plex again with the QR code. Whether both apps can keep a sign-in on the same television at the same time has not been tried (see Known issues). To go back, uninstall MultiPlex; PlxNative is left as it was.

## What's new

- **A new look, modelled on the MultiPlex web app.** The top bar reads Search, Home, Movies, Shows, My list, with icons, then a Settings button and your profile picture at the right end. Library pages have a large title with a title count, filter pills (library, All, genres, More filters) and a seven-column grid that names every card. Detail pages put the poster beside the title, year, length, rating and your stars, with a white Play or Resume pill and a "…" button for the rest (from the beginning, trailer, version, go to show, watched, My list). Settings sections sit on rounded cards, Search has a pill-shaped field, and Watchlist is now called **My list**.
- **A new default player HUD.** The MultiPlex HUD has Back, the title and year at the top with the clock, and at the bottom the progress bar, skip-back, play/pause and skip-forward discs and a **More** pill. The previous HUD is still in Settings as **Classic**. Next to the ⓘ button, small chips show the picture's resolution, HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision or SDR, and the audio codec and channels; a chip the app cannot confirm on this set is dashed with a "?". The ⓘ panel shows video and audio as source and output.
- **Audio modes: Night mode and Clear voices.** Per profile, in the player's More menu and in Settings. Night mode evens out the volume (loud scenes quieter, quiet dialogue louder); Clear voices lifts the dialogue. A mode only works where the app itself has the sound: to make that true for most films, the app decodes AAC, AC-3, E-AC-3, Opus and DTS to stereo while a mode is on, only towards the television's speakers or a Bluetooth soundbar, never towards a receiver over ARC or optical. The sound is then stereo and Atmos is lost. If decoding falls behind, the film goes back to its original sound. **Decode for audio modes** in Settings turns the decoding off.
- **Upcoming episodes on a season page.** After the episodes on your server, the episode strip shows the ones Plex announces that have not come out yet, with their air date or "Air date unknown". They cannot be played.
- **Person pages are rows.** A person's page has one horizontal row per kind of credit (Available on MultiPlex, Acting, Appearances, Producing, Directing, Other credits), moved through with Up and Down like Home.
- **The Magic Remote wheel scrolls rows.** With the pointer over a row, the wheel moves it sideways, two cards per notch; at the end of a row, or over a row that fits on screen, it scrolls the page. Stacked rows on Home, Library, Detail, Search and person pages have more space between them.
- **A person at the top of search results.** When what you typed clearly names a person (the full name, or at least two whole words of it), a card with their photo, jobs and number of titles appears between the field and the results; Down reaches it and OK opens their page.
- **Menus and the profile picker, like the web app.** A card's menu shows the title first, the account menu has an icon per row, and the profile picker shows a lock on a protected profile, an initial for a profile without a photo, and whether a profile is the current one, the owner or a managed profile.
- **The Plex dashboard names this app "MultiPlex (LG TV)".**
- **The Experimental playback engine can take a film on webOS 5 sets** (from upstream). With Settings → Video & playback → Playback engine set to Experimental, a webOS 5.x set now lets the television play an eligible original itself (one audio track, no subtitles, a file that would play directly). This was run upstream on one LG OLED CX with a developer build, not with this package. Standard stays the default.

## Fixed

- **The webOS version could read as unknown** on a boot where the television's usual version file could not be read, which made several playback rules act as for an unknown firmware. On webOS 5 and newer the app now reads the version from a second file (from upstream).

## Privacy: what changed from PlxNative

- **No telemetry.** This build carries no crash-report or analytics destination: the Sentry and PostHog addresses PlxNative's releases compiled in are not in it, so nothing is sent to either, the first launch does not ask about reports, and Settings → Privacy & data shows the privacy policy and Delete all local data without the reporting choices. Crash and event logs stay on the television.
- **No contact with the PlxNative project's servers.** The online HUD gallery and its host, `huds.plxnative.com`, are gone, as are links to plxnative.com. A HUD package can still be installed from the HUD folder on the television or from an address you type.
- **Two new requests to Plex.** A season page asks Plex's metadata service (metadata.provider.plex.tv) for the season's announced episodes, and a search sends what you typed to the same service to find people, both with the profile's Plex account sign-in. Plex processes these under its own privacy policy; nothing reaches us.

## Compatibility

- **Plays video, verified by watching it — no television for this package.** Nothing in this release was installed or run on a television by the people who made it, because no television was available. The playback code is PlxNative's, which its maintainer runs on a 2019 LG 49SM9000PLA (platform release 4.10.2, marketed as webOS 4.5), but that is evidence about PlxNative's packages, not this one.
- **Plays video, reported by someone else — no reports for this package.** PlxNative 0.10.0 has [reports from its own users](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.10.0); they are about a different package and do not carry over to MultiPlex.
- **Starts, and nothing further is known — firmware from webOS 4.0 up.** The release run checks this binary's libraries and symbols against the firmware images webosbrew has from webOS 4.0 up, and the verdict for this exact package is in the [technical audit](https://github.com/Shibeon/MultiPlex-LGTV/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md). That grades startup and says nothing about whether video plays.

If you install it, [tell us what happened](https://github.com/Shibeon/MultiPlex-LGTV/issues), with your television's model and webOS version. It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Known issues

- **Every new feature in this release is unseen on a television.** In particular: the Magic Remote wheel on rows and the new spacing between rows; the audio modes' in-app decoding, whose timing against the picture (lip sync), processor load and fallback to the original sound have not been measured on any television; and the person card in search, which has only been run against the project's test server, not a real Plex Media Server and plex.tv.
- **Running MultiPlex and PlxNative side by side has not been tried.** The app ids differ, so they install as two apps, but both keep their sign-in in the television's app database under the same record name, and whether webOS keeps the two apart has not been checked. MultiPlex also deletes, when it starts, log files left under PlxNative's old names in the television's temporary folder that belong to its own user; if webOS runs both apps as the same user, that can include PlxNative's current logs. If one of them loses its sign-in or cannot save it, uninstall the one you do not use, or tell us.
- **PlxNative 0.10.0's known issues are inherited,** among them direct play that can hang on Buffering on some webOS 5 and newer sets, and some 2019 sets whose Developer Mode sandbox withholds the video device. The list is in [the PlxNative 0.10.0 release note](https://github.com/GLinnik21/plx-native/releases/tag/v0.10.0).
## Help test this release

**If you have an LG webOS television, install it and tell us whether it starts, signs in and plays a film.** **If you use the television's speakers or a Bluetooth soundbar,** turn on Night mode or Clear voices during a film and tell us whether the sound stays in step with the picture. **If you have a Magic Remote,** try the wheel over a row on Home. [Open an issue](https://github.com/Shibeon/MultiPlex-LGTV/issues). It working is as useful a report as it failing.

## Installing

Download **app.multiplex.native_0.11.0_arm.ipk** and install it with the [Homebrew Channel](https://github.com/webosbrew/webos-homebrew-channel) or [dev-manager-desktop](https://github.com/webosbrew/dev-manager-desktop). You do **not** need a rooted TV. MultiPlex is not listed in the Homebrew Channel's catalogue, so download the file from this page.

Nothing in this distribution chain is signed, so this sha256 is what tells you the file you have is the file published here:

```
__IPK_SHA256__  app.multiplex.native_0.11.0_arm.ipk
```

[Installing and checking a download](https://github.com/Shibeon/MultiPlex-LGTV/blob/main/docs/install-and-verify.md) covers the other assets, how to check the hash on each platform, and the Developer Mode expiry that uninstalls your apps. MultiPlex is licensed GPL-3.0-or-later, like PlxNative, and bundles FFmpeg under LGPL-2.1-or-later and the libass subtitle renderer with its own libraries under their licences; the complete corresponding source is attached to this release. Exactly what was built, verified and shipped is in the [technical audit for v0.11.0](https://github.com/Shibeon/MultiPlex-LGTV/blob/main/docs/release-audits/v0.11.0.md).
