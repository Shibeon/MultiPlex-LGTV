its name, **PlxNative**, and its logo and splash artwork as the identity of the original application,

artwork in `assets/logo-master.png`, `assets/splash-master.png`, and the `pkg/icon*.png` /

You may fork and redistribute this software under the GPL-3.0-or-later terms; please do so under your own name
and mark, so that users can tell the projects apart. This is the usual reservation made by projects
that ship an identity along with their code, and it restricts nothing else. It rests on
**trademark** — on the marks identifying the origin of an application — and is not a copyright
